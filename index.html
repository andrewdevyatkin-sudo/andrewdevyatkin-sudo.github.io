Twitch Clipper

A private tool that turns the owner's own clips and animated stories into YouTube Shorts.

What it does

  • Cuts short vertical videos and adds captions, on the owner's own computer.
  • Uploads finished Shorts to the owner's own YouTube channels as private videos. The owner reviews each one and makes it public in YouTube Studio.
  • Shows the owner their own channel's name and view counts.

Who can use it

Only its owner. Twitch Clipper is not a public service: there is no sign-up, and nobody else's account or data is used.

Google sign-in

Twitch Clipper uses Google sign-in only to reach the owner's YouTube channels through the YouTube Data API (upload videos, and read the channel's own name and stats). The login is stored only on the owner's computer. Details are in the privacy policy.
