Privacy Policy

Effective 24 November 2026.

Twitch Clipper is a private tool run by one person (the "owner") for their own YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public.

What Google data it accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, Twitch Clipper asks for two YouTube permissions:

  • youtube.upload: to upload the owner's finished videos to the owner's channel.
  • youtube.readonly: to read the signed-in channel's name and ID (so the right channel is used) and the view, like and comment counts of the owner's own videos.

How the data is used

Only to upload the owner's videos and to show the owner their own channel's numbers. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or training AI models, and it is never sold.

Where it is stored

The Google login (an OAuth token) and the channel numbers are stored only on the owner's own computer. Twitch Clipper runs no server and sends this data to nobody else.

Sharing

Google user data is not shared with or transferred to anyone. Twitch Clipper's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the token file on the owner's computer removes the stored login.
